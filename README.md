DUSTLINE: Station 9 is a round-based tactical FPS in the browser, built on three.js with no build step and no network: two teams of five fight over a freight yard at dusk, the attackers plant a charge at Site A (the fuel depot) or Site B (the signal tower), and every round pays for the next one's guns. Four weapons (sidearm, rifle, marksman rifle, shotgun), a flashbang, kevlar, a buy menu, an economy that rewards kills, plants and round wins, and nine bots that follow lanes, hold angles, investigate gunfire, rush a planted bomb and defuse it if you let them stand there. Hitscan gunplay with headshots, recoil and spread that punish firing on the move, crouching, jumping, and a fixed-step simulation kept apart from the render loop.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to play, B buys, E plants the charge (hold), G throws a flashbang.
